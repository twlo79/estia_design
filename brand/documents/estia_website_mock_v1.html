03The Stay
The First Hour

Your host meets you at the door and hands you the home — how everything works, what's in the fridge, and one good address for tomorrow morning. Twenty minutes later you are alone, and the home is yours. We are one message away, for the whole of your stay.

One host

Every guest has one dedicated host — one person, from your first message to your last morning. Not a front desk. A name.

Before you arrive

The room is warmed to 24 degrees. The lights are on — 2700K, the colour of evening. The door opens on a home, not a unit.

Weekly care

Fresh linen, quiet housekeeping, and a small written note when we've been. You will know the home is looked after, without ever being interrupted.

Chosen, not collected

We don't take every home. We look for good bones — proportion, light, a quiet lane, a warmth of wood — and only then does a door earn the name. Each home keeps its own character.

The quiet

Every home here is different. What never changes is the quiet. Close the door — the city stops.

04The Story

When I was travelling in Greece, I realised something. What stays with you about a country is never the food, the sea, or the buildings. It is the people — the way they welcome you decides whether you return.

I came home to Taipei and saw what was missing. A guest staying a month here chooses between serviced apartments with systems but no soul, and beautiful rentals whose warmth changes with every host. Discipline without warmth — or warmth without discipline.

ESTIA is the middle we could not find, so we built it: the discipline of a hotel, the soul of a home. The name belongs to the Greek goddess of the hearth — the fire at the centre of every house. The fire here is Taiwanese.

ESTIA is Greek in spirit. Taiwanese in hands.
That's the whole idea.

Jim · Founder
