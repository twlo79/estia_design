03The Neighbourhood
The boulevard

Ren'ai Road is Taipei's calmest grand avenue — camphor trees, embassies, and buildings with doormen who remember names. The city's oldest quarters are minutes away on foot.

Mornings

Breakfast is a lane away, the morning market two. The parks fill early with tai-chi and dog-walkers; coffee arrives at a counter where they'll know your order by week two.

Evenings

Old shopfronts, small kitchens that seat ten, and streets that go quiet by eleven. This is the Taipei that residents keep to themselves — you'll live in the middle of it.

Your host's own addresses — the breakfast counter, the bookshop, the late-night noodle house — come with the home.
